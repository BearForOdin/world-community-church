About World Community Church

Who we are

We're a church in Tantra Hills, made up of ordinary people learning together what it looks like to follow Jesus in everyday life.

Our story

Built around a simple conviction

World Community Church exists because we believe every person in Tantra Hills deserves a place where they're known, cared for, and pointed toward Jesus — regardless of where they've come from or what they already believe.

We keep things simple: honest teaching from the Bible, real community instead of anonymity, and practical ways to serve the neighborhood right outside our doors.

What we believe

The convictions that shape us

Scripture

We believe the Bible is God's word and our guide for faith and everyday life.

Grace

We believe every person is welcomed and changed by grace, not by earning their place.

Community

We believe faith grows best in relationship — not in isolation.
