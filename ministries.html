Something for every season of life

From the youngest members of our church family to our small groups meeting across Tantra Hills, there's a place to belong and grow.

Nursery – Primary

Sunday School

Kids belong here too. During Sunday gatherings, children move to age-appropriate classes where they learn Bible stories, sing, play, and start building friendships with other kids in Tantra Hills.

Every child is checked in and out securely, and our team is trained to keep kids safe and cared for.
